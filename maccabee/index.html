Hometown Hive: Charleston, South Carolina

The music Hive: Nashville, Tennessee

The Bee is an artist, producer, engineer, mixer, live sound engineer, and teacher. He first began his musical endeavors in 6th grade, playing the trombone. He was last chair, but with hard work and determination, he reached first chair by seventh grade. These qualities of practice and discipline is what makes Maccabee stand out amongst the rest. During high school, Mac started rapping on beats he found on Youtube. He recorded his vocals with a cheap USB microphone he found at a pawn shop. Twists of fate brought Maccabee to study audio engineering at Middle Tennessee State University, where he created a stable foundation to build his music production career here in Nashville, TN. He's been utilizing his experience to record, engineer, mix, master, and teach live sound. His expertise and relaxed personality make him a great addition to your musical projects.
